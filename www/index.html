How to play

Left side drag – move / steer swings
Right side drag – look around
Hold SWING – shoot a web & swing. Release to let go. Keep holding to cruise.
Release at the top of the arc – "Perfect release" speed boost
JUMP while swinging – leap off with a flip
Hold JUMP in the air – dive to build speed
Tap JUMP in the air – do a trick
Run into a wall – wall-run. Look up to climb, look sideways to run along it, wrap around corners
JUMP on a wall – kick off toward where you look. JUMP as you clear a ledge to launch
ZIP – zip to the ledge under the crosshair (JUMP on landing = point-launch)
ZIP in mid-air – web-zip forward for a burst of speed
Look while swinging – the swing curves toward where you look
TRICK (in the air) – stick forward = front flip, back = backflip, left/right = barrel roll, no stick = spins, Superman, Starfish, Cannonball, Corkscrew…
Chain tricks – more tricks in one jump multiply the points. Land mid-trick and you bail!
Jobs – stand in an orange 🍕 or blue 📰 ring and tap the button
Pizza – deliver on time; hard crashes ruin the pizza
Papers – THROW auto-aims at the nearest subscriber
🎈 Balloons – 40 hidden around the city
